# Privacy Policy for WatchMyMoney

**Last updated: October 22, 2026**

## Introduction
WatchMyMoney ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Wear OS application handles your information.

## Data Collection and Use
**We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers.**

WatchMyMoney functions entirely as an offline standalone application on your Wear OS device.

### Local Data Storage
The application stores the following user-provided preferences locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore:
*   **Annual Salary**: Used solely to calculate and display your real-time earnings.
*   **Currency Symbol**: Used for display formatting.
*   **Reset Hour**: Used to determine when the daily counter resets.

This data remains strictly on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the application or clear application data.

## Third-Party Services
*   **No Analytics**: We do not use third-party analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase) to track your usage.
*   **No Advertising**: This application does not display advertisements and does not share data with advertising networks.
*   **Zero Network Permissions**: The application does not declare or use `android.permission.INTERNET`.

## Permissions
The application requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for Wear OS system integration:
*   `BIND_COMPLICATION_PROVIDER`: To allow the Wear OS watch face system to display your earnings in complications.
*   *Zero WakeLocks*: The application does not hold background WakeLocks and complies with Wear OS ambient power guidelines.

## Children's Privacy
Our application does not collect any personal information from children. It is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
